### Projects with Confirmed Follow-up Publications

**Project 7 – BayBE One More Time (Corrosion Inhibitors).** The project used BayBE, an actively maintained open-source Bayesian back end for design of experiments by EMD group/Merck KGaA (github.com/emdgroup/baybe). BayBE has continued active development and maintenance well after March 2024, constituting a concrete maintained software package on the same topic (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 9-10, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 17-18).

**Project 14 – Bayesian optimization of likely negative candidates in imbalanced biological datasets.** Lead Mayk Caldas Ramos subsequently published: Ramos, M. C., Michtavy, S. S., White, A. D., Porosoff, M. D. "Bayesian Optimization of Catalysis with In-Context Learning." *ACS Central Science* 12, 599–615 (April 2026). DOI: 10.1021/acscentsci.5c02418 (ramos2026bayesianoptimizationof pages 1-3). This extends BO for challenging chemical optimization problems, a thematic continuation from the hackathon project on BO in difficult data regimes.

**Project 15 – Adaptive Batch Sizes for Bayesian Optimization of Reaction Yield.** Hackathon team members Rubén Laplaza and Jan Weinreich co-authored: Schoepfer, A. A., Weinreich, J., Laplaza, R., Waser, J., Corminboeuf, C. "Cost-informed Bayesian reaction optimization." *Digital Discovery* 3, 2289–2297 (October 2024). DOI: 10.1039/d4dd00225c (schoepfer2024costinformedbayesianreaction pages 6-7, schoepfer2024costinformedbayesianreaction pages 1-2). This paper addresses cost-aware experiment planning in reaction BO, directly related to the hackathon project's theme of optimizing batching strategies for reaction yield optimization.

**Project 21 – Benchmarking MolDAIS.** Lead Farshud Sorourifar published: Sorourifar, F., Banker, T., Paulson, J. A. "Adaptive subspace Bayesian optimization over molecular descriptor libraries for data-efficient chemical design." *Digital Discovery* 4(10), 2910–2926 (2025). DOI: 10.1039/d5dd00188a (sorourifar2025adaptivesubspacebayesian pages 15-17, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 11-12). This is a direct continuation of the MolDAIS (Molecular Descriptors with Actively Identified Subspaces) approach developed during the hackathon.

**Project 24 – ScattBO Benchmark.** Lead Andy Anker's team published a related software paper cited in the hackathon compilation: Johansen, F. L., Anker, A. S., Christiansen, T. L., Voss, L. S., Juhás, P., Billinge, S. J. L., Jensen, K. M. Ø. "A GPU-Accelerated Open-Source Python Package for Calculating Powder Diffraction, Small-Angle-, and Total Scattering with the Debye Scattering Equation." *Journal of Open Source Software* 9, 6024 (2024) (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 18-19). The ScattBO benchmark itself continues as an actively maintained open-source package.

**Project 35 – Tutorial for GAUCHE.** The GAUCHE library, developed by team member Ryan-Rhys Griffiths and collaborators, appeared in: Griffiths, R.-R., Klarner, L., Moss, H., Ravuri, A., Truong, S., Du, Y., Stanton, S., et al. "Gauche: A library for Gaussian processes in chemistry." *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems*, Vol. 36 (2024) (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 14-15, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 18-19). The hackathon project extended GAUCHE with input warping tutorials; GAUCHE remains actively maintained.

**Project 36 – Scalable Nonmyopic Bayesian Optimization in Dynamic Cost Settings.** Lead Sang T. Truong published: Truong, S. T., Nguyen, D. Q., Neiswanger, W., Griffiths, R.-R., Ermon, S., Haber, N., Koyejo, S. "Neural Nonmyopic Bayesian Optimization in Dynamic Cost Settings." arXiv:2601.06505 (January 2026). DOI: 10.48550/arxiv.2601.06505 (truong2026neuralnonmyopicbayesian pages 12-13). This is a direct continuation of the same nonmyopic cost-aware BO theme from the hackathon project.

**Project 41 – RAMBO I (Retrieval Augmented Initialization for Bayesian Optimization).** Lead Bojana Ranković and collaborator Philippe Schwaller published: Ranković, B. and Schwaller, P. "GOLLuM: Gaussian Process Optimized LLMs – Reframing LLM Finetuning through Bayesian Optimization." arXiv:2504.06265 (April 2025). DOI: 10.48550/arxiv.2504.06265 (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 15-16, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 3-6). This continues the LLM-plus-Bayesian-optimization research direction initiated in the RAMBO hackathon project, though the specific focus has evolved from retrieval-augmented initialization to deep kernel GP-LLM architectures.

**Project 45 – Bayesian Optimization for Generality.** Lead Felix Strieth-Kalthoff and hackathon co-participant Mohammad Haddadnia published: Haddadnia, M., Grashoff, L., Strieth-Kalthoff, F. "BoTier: Multi-Objective Bayesian Optimization with Tiered Objective Structures." *Digital Discovery* 4, 1417–1422 (January 2025). DOI: 10.1039/d5dd00039d (haddadnia2025botiermultiobjectivebayesian pages 2-3, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 6-7). This addresses hierarchical/tiered multi-objective BO, closely related to the hackathon project's focus on finding general parameters across multiple related tasks.
