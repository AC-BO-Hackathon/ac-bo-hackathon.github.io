## Works Arising From, Acknowledging, or Citing the AC BO Hackathon 2024

### Background

The Acceleration Consortium Bayesian Optimization Hackathon for Chemistry and Materials (AC BO Hackathon 2024) was a two-day virtual event held on March 27–28, 2024, organized by the Acceleration Consortium (University of Toronto) with Merck KGaA (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 2-3). The event brought together 139 active participants across 39 teams from 52 academic institutions, 3 national laboratories, and 14 companies, located in 59 cities, 19 countries, and 4 continents (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 3-6). Participants developed Bayesian optimization algorithms, applied them to benchmarking tasks, designed new benchmarks, created tutorials, and described real-world applications in chemistry and materials science (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 2-3). The hackathon produced 45 team projects archived on GitHub (github.com/AC-BO-Hackathon) and Zenodo (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 6-7, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 16-17). Note: the user's question states the event was held 4–5 March 2024, but the hackathon manuscript itself records the dates as March 27–28, 2024.

### (a) Works That Explicitly Name the Hackathon

**1. Baird, S.G., Ansari, M., Afzal, Z., Ai, Q., Al-Feghali, A., Alain, M., ... Zou, Y. (2025). "Bayesian Optimization Hackathon for Chemistry and Materials." ChemRxiv. DOI: 10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-dzh5z.**

- **Publication date:** June 2025 (ChemRxiv preprint, not yet peer-reviewed).
- **Connection:** This is the hackathon manuscript itself—the definitive record of the event. It documents all 45 projects, describes the event's organization, participants, and outputs, and links to project pages at https://ac-bo-hackathon.github.io/ (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 2-3, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 3-6). The paper has over 100 co-authors (all hackathon participants) and describes projects spanning drug discovery molecular representations, electromagnetic interference shielding, corrosion inhibitors, MOF selection for CO₂ capture, zeolite synthesis, robust Gaussian processes for concrete science, input warping within the GAUCHE library, transfer learning in BO, multi-agent LLM systems for reaction optimization (RAMBO), and numerous others (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 9-10, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 13-14, baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 16-17). The manuscript self-reports 2 citations at time of retrieval, but these citing works could not be identified in the databases I searched, likely because the paper was deposited only in June 2025 (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 6-7).
- **Connection strength:** Strongest possible — this is the hackathon paper itself.

This is the only work I could verify as explicitly naming the AC BO Hackathon 2024.

### Interpretation

The AC BO Hackathon 2024 is at a very early stage of bibliometric impact. The primary hackathon manuscript was deposited on ChemRxiv only in June 2025—more than a year after the event—and has not yet been peer-reviewed or formally published in a journal (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 2-3). The manuscript self-reports 2 citations, but these could not be identified in the databases searched, suggesting they may be very recent works not yet indexed (baird2025bayesianoptimizationhackathon pages 6-7). The 45 team projects are archived on GitHub and Zenodo, but none has yet spun off into a standalone published paper, dataset paper, or software-release paper that explicitly names the hackathon. Several works by hackathon participants on closely related Bayesian optimization topics exist (GOLLuM, BoTier, BayBE, multi-fidelity BO), and some share overlapping personnel, but none explicitly cite or acknowledge the AC BO Hackathon event. Given the recency of the hackathon manuscript, additional citing works are likely to appear in the coming months.
